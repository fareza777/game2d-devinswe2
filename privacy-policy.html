Oathfire RPG: Chapter 1

Privacy Policy · Last updated September 22, 2026

Oathfire RPG: Chapter 1 is an offline single-player role-playing game. This policy explains what information the game and its service providers process when you install or play it.

Information stored by the game

Game progress, settings, and the Remove Ads entitlement are stored locally on your device. The game does not require an account, collect your name, contacts, messages, microphone recordings, photos, or precise location, and does not operate a first-party user profile or social feed.

Advertising

With your consent where required, Oathfire uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to show banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads. Google may process device identifiers, ad interaction information, approximate location, and diagnostics to provide, measure, and protect advertising. Google’s processing is governed by its policies and controls, available at Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob Help.

Purchases

The game offers a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase called Remove Ads. Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Oathfire receives the purchase result and entitlement needed to disable ads; payment card and billing details are handled by Google Play and are not collected by the game.

Children

Oathfire is a fantasy role-playing game intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and choices

Local game data remains until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall the game. You can manage advertising consent through the consent dialog when it is shown and manage purchases through Google Play. Removing the app stops its local processing.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The “Last updated” date identifies the current version. For privacy questions, use the developer contact shown on the Oathfire RPG: Chapter 1 Google Play listing.
